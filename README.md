# The DM's Satchel

A toolkit for running fifth-edition tabletop games, plus a shared world wiki that several GMs can build together.

- **Table Tools**: NPCs (with ancestry, role, look, voice, wants and secrets), a quest builder, factions/guilds/clans,
  names for people, towns, taverns, shops, ships and guilds, settlements/taverns/shops, sensory descriptions,
  rumors, plot hooks, trinkets and loot by challenge tier. Reroll any single line with ↻.
- **Character Forge**: a complete character under the 2024 rules at any level from 1 to 20: species and lineage,
  class and subclass, background with its ability bonuses, origin feat, skills and tools, ability scores (standard
  array, point buy or 4d6), level-up feats and ASIs, HP, AC, attacks, spellcasting and slots, class resources and
  features by level. Every character comes with a life story in the style of DCUGen's lifepaths: family and standing,
  upbringing and childhood events, why they took up their background and class, adult life, the people in their
  life, personality, goals, secrets, regrets and hooks for the DM. Each section rerolls on its own, characters are
  seeded so the same link always shows the same character, and sheets can be copied, downloaded, printed or shared.
- **World**: a wiki for one shared campaign world: regions, settlements, locations, factions, guilds, clans and
  houses, NPCs, player characters, deities, items, a timeline of events, quests and lore. Entries nest
  ("located in"), connect to each other ("member of", "enemy of"…), link by name with `[[Entry Name]]`, and carry
  GM-only secrets players never see. Anything from the table tools or the Character Forge can be sent to the world
  with **Add to world**. Every change records who made it; if two GMs edit the same entry, the second save is
  stopped instead of overwriting the first.
- **Places that work like hubs**: regions hold areas and towns, towns hold taverns, shops and dungeons. Every place
  has tabs for its **People** (roll one NPC or populate a whole town with residents suited to it, keep the ones you
  like, move existing NPCs in or out), its **Quest board** (a corkboard of jobs: roll or write quests and move them
  from Rumor to Active to Completed in one click), **Places** inside it (random towns, areas, taverns and shops),
  **Powers** (guilds, factions and families based there) and **Maps**.
- **Atlas**: the whole world as a tree of places with people and open jobs counted on every row, and quick-add buttons.
- **Build console** (Admin → Build, open to every GM): add quests, key NPCs, NPCs, factions, guilds, houses,
  regions, areas, towns, locations, deities, items, events and lore from one form. "Random" fills it all in, the
  place you pick is remembered for the next one, and places can be pinned on a map straight after saving.
  **Key NPCs** are starred on the world's front page and listed first in their town.
- **Map pins manager** (Admin → Map pins): every pin on every map in one table, editable in place, plus a list of
  places not yet on any map.
- **Maps**: upload a world, city or dungeon map (or link one) and drop pins for places, lairs and points of interest.
  Click a pin to open a side panel for that place with its people, quest board and sub-places, and roll NPCs or quests
  right there. A new pin can create its town or area in the same step. Pins of places that have their own map open
  that map, so you can drill from the world to a region to a city. Pan, zoom and pinch on phones.
- **Relationship web**: a live graph of how factions, people and places connect, built from each entry's
  connections. Filter by type, focus on one entry and its neighbors, drag things around.
- **Session log**: each GM logs their sessions (number, date, GM, players, XP, loot, what happened). New sessions
  number themselves, and every entry shows which sessions it appeared in.
- **Party standing**: the party's reputation with every faction, guild, house, god, town or person, from sworn
  enemies (−5) to champions (+5), with a dated history of what changed it and which session it happened in.
- **Family trees**: parents, children, spouses and siblings, drawn as a tree for every noble house and clan.
- **Rumor mill**: gossip starts in a town and spreads to its neighbors, changing a little with each retelling.
- **Who stands where**: a grid of every faction, guild and house against every other, with tensions called out.
- **Faction clocks**: plans in motion (a cult's ritual, a guild's takeover) as clocks you fill between sessions; one
  click runs a faction turn.
- **Session prep**: one page before each session with last time's notes, where the party is and who's there, quests
  in play, clocks about to finish, upcoming dates, the party's HP, loose threads and a shared GM scratch pad.
- **Table talk**: players and GMs comment on quests, handouts, rumors and anything else players can see; GMs can
  hide or remove comments. A feed shows the latest across the world.
- **GM discussions**: GMs talk on any entry ("I'm bringing her back in session 14") and resolve threads when
  they're settled. An inbox lists every open thread. Players never see any of it.
- **Search everything**: Ctrl+K (⌘K) or `/` opens one search box for tools, world entries, classes, species and feats.
  Press `?` for keyboard shortcuts (`g` then a letter jumps between pages).
- **Many worlds, GMs and players**: anyone signed in can create a world and becomes its owner. Each world has a GM
  invite link and a player invite link; opening one asks for a name and hands out a personal sign-in code. Switch
  worlds from the menu at the top. Worlds can be private (members only) or open (anyone can read).
- **Players add to the world**: players add their own characters and journal entries, and suggest anything else
  (an NPC, a rumor, a place). Suggestions wait for a GM to approve, edit or reject them.
- **Character sheets**: characters from the Forge keep their full sheet in the world, with a play tracker the
  player and GMs share: hit points, conditions, spell slots, hit dice, rests, coins, inventory and notes. Level up
  in one click.
- **What my character knows**: players mark the people, places and secrets their character has learned, with notes.
- **The Party**: who's in the party, the shared treasury (coins and items, with a loot log anyone can add to and an
  even split), current quests, handouts the GM reveals at the table, and the party journal.
- **Encounters & travel**: fights fitted to the party with the 2024 encounter budget (stat blocks open on D&D
  Beyond), chance meetings, weather with its rules, a saved encounter table per area, and travel times between map
  pins once a map has a scale.
- **Dungeons & lairs**: a keyed site in one roll, with monsters fitted to the party, traps, puzzles, treasure and a
  map; GMs can add it to the world with a page per room and a pin on each.
- **Import notes**: bring in Markdown files, an Obsidian folder, or pasted notes, with a preview before anything
  is saved.
- **Export & print**: the world, a region or one kind of entry as Markdown or a printable page, as a GM copy or a
  player-safe copy.
- **Works offline**: install it like an app; the table tools, Character Forge and Reference work without a
  connection.
- **Calendar**: your world's own months, weekdays, moons and holidays. GMs move time forward; events, sessions and
  journals with dates appear on the month grid.
- **More pictures**: a gallery of up to 12 captioned pictures on any entry, with a full-size viewer.
- **Pictures**: upload a portrait, a town sketch or a handout image to any entry (or paste a link).
- **Discord**: connect your group's channel and post session recaps and handouts with one click.
- **Your library**: short notes on monsters, items, classes, subclasses, species, feats and spells from the books
  your group owns. Bestiary monsters turn up in encounters and dungeons; your items in loot and shops; your classes,
  species, subclasses, backgrounds and feats in the Character Forge (a class form covers hit die, proficiencies, features by level and
  spellcasting, so the Forge builds the whole character); your spells join class spell lists in the Forge, NPC
  spellcasting and the Spells reference.
- **NPC table cards**: print a card per NPC (voice, manner, wants, stats, attitudes, secret) for the people nearby.
- **Saved encounters**: keep a rolled encounter where the party will meet it; session prep reminds you.
- **Printable session sheet**: everything for the table on one page, from session prep.
- **Member profiles**: everyone adds their pronouns and a short note for the table, shown on the party page and beside their characters.
- **Party bonds**: players tie their characters to each other (rivals, siblings, debts…), shown on the party page.
- **Attendance and XP**: record who came and XP or milestones per session; the party page shows who's due a level.
- **Session recaps**: one click drafts the next session-log entry from everything that changed since the last one.
- **Since your last visit**: opening a world shows what others changed while you were away.
- **Quest objectives**: a checklist of steps per quest, some secret until revealed, with progress on the boards.
- **Your own roll tables**: write random tables (weights, dice, choices, tables inside tables) and roll them in play.
- **What needs writing**: a GM list of thin spots (undescribed entries, empty places, flat NPCs, quests with no steps).
- **Spreadsheet import**: bring in a CSV of NPCs, items or places, say which column is which, and check a preview first.
- **Entry templates**: save any entry as a template and start new ones from it.
- **Quick notes**: jot a journal note from any page (button or the j key), linked to what you're looking at.
- **Keep going offline**: character sheet changes and private notes made without a connection are kept and sent when it comes back.
- **Private notes**: everyone can keep their own notes on any page, seen by nobody else.
- **Attitudes**: how each NPC and power feels about each character, on both pages and in session prep.
- **Quest rewards**: completing a quest offers to put its reward (coin and items read from the text) into the party treasury.
- **Print a character**: a clean printable page for any world character, from their sheet and play state.
- **Hand it over**: give an item or coin from your character to another party member; both sheets update at once.
- **Split the loot**: share the party treasury's coin between characters, by shares, straight onto their sheets.
- **Time and light**: the in-world hour during play and the torches and spells burning down, moved on by the GM and seen by everyone.
- **Quick polls**: anyone asks the table a question with a few choices; everyone votes and sees the tally.
- **Notices**: GMs post short notices ("No game this Friday") on the front page, shown as a banner until dismissed or expired.
- **Suggest an edit**: players propose changes to entries they can't edit; GMs see the difference and accept or decline.
- **Handouts for one player**: GMs give a hidden handout (or any entry) to chosen players only.
- **Show to the table**: a GM pops a handout (or any entry) up on every player's screen.
- **Next session**: propose a few dates, everyone answers yes / maybe / no, lock in the best one and post it to
  Discord. The date shows on the world's home page.
- **Downtime**: players file what their characters do between sessions; GMs write how it went, and it stays on
  the character's page.
- **Measure on the map**: click points along a route on any map with a scale to see the miles and travel time at each pace.
- **The party on the map**: a ⚑ token shows where the party is on every map with that place (or a place around it) pinned.
- **Your own map markers**: drop markers with notes on any map; private unless you show one to the party.
- **Fog of war**: paint over the parts of a map the party hasn't explored; players see only what's revealed.
- **Light or dark**: a header switch for light, dark or automatic (follows your device), remembered per browser.
- **Members-only site**: one switch makes every page and tool need a personal code or invite link.
- **See it as a player**: GMs preview the world exactly as players see it, then go back with one click.
- **Hidden entries**: GMs can keep any entry hidden from players while they prep, then reveal it in one click.
- **Who shows up where**: NPC pages say when the party last met them; session prep lists people nearby who could turn up again.
- **Spotlight**: session prep shows which characters the recent session logs name least, with their goals as hooks.
- **Character goals**: players write short and long-term goals (some kept from the party); GMs see them in session prep.
- **Secrets and clues**: a running list in session prep of things the party could find out; tick them off as they come out.
- **Start here**: GMs pick the entries newcomers should read first; they lead the front page and the session zero page.
- **Session zero**: a primer every member reads (pitch, tone, house rules, safety tools, lines and veils); players tell the GMs privately what to keep out, optionally without their name.
- **Your own fields**: GMs add extra fields to any kind of entry (shared or GM-only), shown on the form and the page.
- **Follow an entry**: hear about changes others make to the entries you care about.
- **Recently viewed**: the entries you opened last, in the sidebar and at the top of Ctrl+K.
- **Favorites**: star entries you keep going back to; they sit at the top of your world sidebar.
- **Is that name taken?**: the entry form warns when the world already has an entry with that name, or a close one.
- **Make a copy**: GMs duplicate any entry and edit the copy; a place can bring its people, shops and sub-places along.
- **Say it right**: a pronunciation beside any name, shown on its page, NPC cards and the session sheet, with an A–Z guide for the world.
- **Where the party has been**: each move of the party is kept with the in-world date; maps draw the route so far and the party page lists the journey.
- **Wish lists**: players note what their characters hope to find or earn; GMs see them in session prep and beside matching loot.
- **Read-aloud in prep**: session prep and the printable sheet gather the “> ” boxes from what's planned, in order, ready to read.
- **Roll a detail**: “Roll” beside an NPC's looks, personality, voice, wants and secret, a settlement's trouble or landmarks, and a place's features fills the field from the app's tables.
- **The world in numbers**: entries, words, sessions, the most talked-about NPCs, the busiest places, who has written the most and other records.
- **Contents for long entries**: three or more “## ” headings in a description give a small table of contents that jumps to each part.
- **Picture wall**: every picture in the world on one page (portraits, places, galleries, maps), by kind and tag.
- **Previously on…**: the front page opens with the last session's summary and where the party left off.
- **Birthdays on the calendar**: characters' and NPCs' in-world birthdays recur every year on the calendar and in session prep.
- **Mood music**: a music or ambience link on any entry; places inherit it from the place around them, and session prep offers it for where the party is.
- **Reactions**: 👍 😂 😮 ❤️ 🎲 on table talk and quotes, with who reacted.
- **Quote wall**: memorable lines from the table, with who said them and when; a random one greets you on the front page.
- **Ask the GM**: a private thread between each player and the GMs for questions between sessions, with a badge and a toast when something new arrives.
- **Hall of heroes**: characters who died, retired or went missing, with how and when, their player's epitaph and the sessions they played.
- **Likely duplicates**: a GM page of entries that may be the same thing, to merge or mark as different.
- **Merge two entries**: GMs fold a duplicate into the entry they keep; texts, tags, connections, what's inside it, mentions, talk, standing and map pins move over, and the spare goes to the bin.
- **Many at once**: GMs tick entries on any list or search and reveal, hide, tag, untag or move them into a place together.
- **Tag pages**: a list of every tag with counts, and a description on each tag's page.
- **Search the talk**: world search also finds words in table talk, GM discussions (GMs) and your own notes, and jumps to the comment.
- **Link the mentions**: entry pages show GMs names written as plain text that could be links, and link them in one click.
- **Formatting**: **bold**, *italic*, lists, small headings and "> read-aloud" boxes in any long text.
- **Link suggestions**: typing [[ in any text box suggests entry names; pick one to finish the link.
- **Admin**: world owners manage members and roles, rotate invite links, name the world, see recent activity, and
  download or import a full backup. The site admin can see every world and every account.
- **Reference**: classes with features by level, species, backgrounds, feats and spell slot tables.

Players read what the GMs have revealed; GMs build, edit and see secrets. Each person's role is per world, so you
can be the GM of one campaign and a player in a friend's.
The session notebook keeps each DM's personal saves in their own browser.

## Setup on Vercel

The site is static files plus a few serverless functions in `api/`. There is no build step.

1. **Import the repo** into Vercel (Framework preset: Other). The table tools, Character Forge and Reference work
   right away.
2. **Add a database**: in the Vercel project, open *Storage* → *Create* → **Neon** (Postgres), and connect it to the
   project. This sets `DATABASE_URL`. Tables are created automatically on the first request.
3. **Set the admin code**: in *Settings* → *Environment Variables*, add `ADMIN_CODE` with a long random value
   (for example from a password manager). Redeploy.
4. Open the site, choose **Sign in**, and enter the admin code. Go to **Admin** → **Members & invites** and send the
   GM link to your co-GMs and the player link to your players. Each person picks a name and gets a personal code
   (shown once) to sign in on other devices. Changing `ADMIN_CODE` later changes the admin login.

## Local preview

```
npm install
npm run dev     # static preview at http://localhost:5173 (the shared world needs Vercel and the database)
npm test
```

`vercel dev` runs the API locally too, if you have the Vercel CLI and a `DATABASE_URL`.
